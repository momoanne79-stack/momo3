My First 3-D Movie

처음 본 3D 영화에서 느낀 놀라움과 흥분을 읽어 봅시다.

📖 Reading Passage
Today I watched my first 3-D movie, Alice in Wonderland. When I sat down in the movie theater, I put on special 3-D glasses. At first, I felt a bit dizzy, so I took off my 3-D glasses. But the screen showed two images instead of one. I decided to put my 3-D glasses back on. Then I got a big surprise. The 3-D images on the screen looked amazing! The characters seemed like living things. They seemed to come out of the screen and into the theater. When Alice fell into the rabbit hole, I felt like I was falling with her. During the movie, my heart was beating fast with excitement. Now I love 3-D movies!
